## 🎯 THE CHANGES EXPLAINED SIMPLY

### What is SEOHelmet?
It's a React component that automatically updates page titles, descriptions, and metadata. You just wrap it around your page content:

```jsx
<SEOHelmet
  title="Your Page Title"
  description="Page description..."
  keywords="keyword1, keyword2"
/>
```

### What about the files?
- **robots.txt** - Tells Google which pages to crawl
- **sitemap.xml** - Shows Google all your pages and their importance
- **Meta tags** - Help Google understand your page
- **Structured data** - Shows ratings, reviews, and organization info

### Why does this matter?
- Higher ranking in Google search results
- Better appearance in search results (with stars, descriptions)
- More clicks from people searching for your services
- Better mobile experience
- Faster page loads
